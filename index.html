You Are Cordially Invited to Thanksgiving at the Tokar’s

Pull up a chair at our family table for a warm, joyful Saturday filled with the crackle of autumn, friendly living-room cheering over afternoon football, and the unmistakable comfort of homemade favorites straight from the oven.
